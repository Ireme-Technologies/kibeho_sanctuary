## 10.3 What decision-makers should verify in the demo

1. Can a secretary publish a news item without IT?  
2. Can French and English versions of one article be edited on one screen?  
3. Can a menu link be changed and appear on the public site?  
4. Can an image be replaced in under five minutes?  
5. Are hosting and admin clearly under Diocese control—not a black-box vendor portal?

If those answers are yes, the technical approach meets the spirit of their request—even though it is not WordPress.
